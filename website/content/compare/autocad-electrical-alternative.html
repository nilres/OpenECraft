A free, open source alternative to AutoCAD Electrical

AutoCAD Electrical adds electrical controls design to AutoCAD. OpenECraft is a program of its own for the same work: device-oriented schematics, reports and the panel in 3D, free and on any operating system. It is young, and this page says where.

Why people look at OpenECraft

  • One program and one project file for the schematic, the reports, the panel layout in 3D and the workshop.
  • It runs on Windows, Linux and macOS and in the browser, built from the same code.
  • Every function is a command of the command line and of the MCP server, so scripts and AI agents can do what you can.
  • It is free for everyone: no licenses to count, no accounts, and the source code is public.

What comes across

  • Drawings: DXF and DWG files are imported as graphics on a schematic page, and every page can be exported as DXF or DWG.
  • Parts come in from CSV, BMEcat, EPLAN parts XML and EDZ files, with STEP models for the panel.
  • Projects of other electrical CAD programs cannot be imported yet; a project is drawn anew in OpenECraft.

Where OpenECraft is still young

  • It is a new project and has not been proven in years of production use the way the established tools have. Try it on a real project of yours before you rely on it.
  • It comes with a small set of manufacturer catalogs, far from the large data portals of the commercial vendors; parts from EDZ, BMEcat and STEP files fill the gap.
  • Its symbol libraries and editing tools grow with every release, but users of the established tools will still miss functions they are used to.
  • Help comes from the documentation and from the issue tracker of the project.

Which one fits?

AutoCAD Electrical

When your customers ask for its project files, your company has standardised on it, or you need the support and the part data of its vendor.

OpenECraft

When you want schematics, reports, panel layout in 3D and the workshop in one free program, on any operating system or in the browser and open to scripts and AI agents, and a young project suits you.
