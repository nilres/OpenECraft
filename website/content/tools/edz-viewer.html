EDZ and parts file viewer

What it reads

  • EDZ archives as manufacturers offer them for download, with the data of their parts.
  • EPLAN parts XML, BMEcat catalogs and the CSV and JSON parts files of OpenECraft.
  • The table shows the main data of at most 500 parts; the CSV and JSON files have all parts with all their data.

OpenECraft imports the same files into its parts database, with the symbols and the 3D models of EDZ archives, and assigns the parts to the devices of the schematic.
