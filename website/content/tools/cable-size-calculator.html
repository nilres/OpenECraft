How it is calculated

  • The current-carrying capacity is the one of IEC 60204-1, table 6, for installation method B2: copper conductors with PVC insulation in a duct or conduit at 40 °C, from 0.75 to 120 mm².
  • Another ambient temperature, XLPE insulation, aluminium and circuits bunched together correct it, the bunching after IEC 60364-5-52, table B.52.17.
  • The voltage drop is calculated as in the voltage drop calculator, with the conductor at operating temperature.
  • The larger of the two cross-sections is the one to take. The short-circuit withstand and the protective device can ask for more.

In a project, OpenECraft sizes the wires behind every protective device this way and reports conductors that are too thin in the project check.
