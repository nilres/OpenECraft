Convert DWG to PDF

Turn a DWG or DXF drawing into a PDF that anyone can open and print, without a CAD program. The drawing is converted in your browser and is not uploaded.

Save as

How it works

  1. Choose the drawing or drop it on this page. It is read in your browser and shown here.
  2. Press PDF beside Save as. The PDF has the drawing on one page, with its lines and texts as vectors.
  3. The file is written on your computer. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no limit on the number of files.

What it reads

  • Lines, polylines, circles, arcs, ellipses, splines, texts and blocks of DXF and DWG drawings.
  • What it does not read, such as hatches and pictures, is listed below the drawing.
  • The drawing is put on a page as OpenECraft imports it into a project: a drawing larger than the sheet is scaled to fit.
