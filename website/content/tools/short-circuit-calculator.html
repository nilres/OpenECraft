How high the short-circuit current is at the supply and what is left of it at the end of a cable: the highest current for the breaking capacity, the lowest for the tripping of the protective device.

How it is calculated

  • After IEC 60909-0 for a radial low-voltage network: the voltage factor is 1.1 for the highest and 0.95 for the lowest current.
  • A transformer gives its impedance from its rated power and its short-circuit voltage, with the correction factor of the standard; the network before it is taken as infinitely strong.
  • The cable adds its resistance, at 20 °C for the highest current and at operating temperature for the lowest, and a reactance of 0.08 mΩ per metre.
  • The lowest fault current is the one between a line and the protective conductor of the same cross-section, in a TN system.
  • The peak current follows from the ratio of resistance and reactance at the place of the fault.

In a project, OpenECraft calculates these currents for every protective device of the schematic and checks breaking capacity, disconnection time, the withstand of the cables and selectivity.
