How it is calculated

ΔU = k · I · L · (ρ / A · cos φ + x · sin φ)

  • k is 2 for a single-phase or DC circuit, where the current flows there and back, and 1.732, the square root of 3, for a three-phase circuit with the voltage between the lines.
  • The resistivity is the one of the conductor in operation, 1.25 times its value at 20 °C (IEC 60364-5-52): 0.0225 Ω·mm²/m for copper and 0.036 Ω·mm²/m for aluminium.
  • The reactance of the cable is taken as 0.08 mΩ per metre. It matters for large cross-sections and a low power factor.
  • The limit is yours to set: 3 % is common for lighting and 5 % for other loads, and the regulations of your country decide.

In a project, OpenECraft measures the cable lengths along the installation zones of the rooms and checks every final circuit this way, feeder cables included.
