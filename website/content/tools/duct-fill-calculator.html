Cable duct and conduit fill calculator

How full a wiring duct or a conduit gets: the cross sections of the wires with their insulation and of the cables against the room inside. OpenECraft counts the ducts of a panel the same way.

How it is calculated

  • A single-core wire with PVC insulation is taken as 1 + 1.6 × √A millimetres across, A being its cross-section in mm²: about 3 mm for 1.5 mm².
  • The fill level is the sum of the circles of all wires and cables divided by the room inside: width times depth of a duct, the circle of a conduit.
  • Round wires never fill a duct whole. OpenECraft warns at 70 % by default, and many shops plan new panels with less to leave room for changes.
  • For conduits the regulations of many countries set lower limits, often 40 % for three wires and more.

In the panel layout of OpenECraft the wires are routed through the ducts of the mounting plate, and every duct shows its fill level at its fullest stretch.
