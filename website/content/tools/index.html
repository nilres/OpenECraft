Tools

Free tools that run in your browser

Single functions of OpenECraft for a value or a file of your own. They run in your browser with the code of the editor: nothing is uploaded, and nothing needs an account.

Tools

Voltage drop calculator

The voltage drop of a cable from current, length and cross-section, with the smallest cross-section that keeps your limit.

Calculate the voltage drop

Cable size calculator

The smallest cross-section that carries a current where it is installed and keeps the voltage drop.

Size a cable

Short-circuit current calculator

The short-circuit current at a transformer or supply point and at the end of a cable.

Calculate the short-circuit current

Cable duct and conduit fill calculator

How full a wiring duct or a conduit gets with your wires and cables.

Calculate the fill level

DXF and DWG viewer

Look at a DXF or DWG drawing and save it as SVG, PDF or the other of the two formats.

Open the DXF and DWG viewer

Convert DWG to PDF

A DWG or DXF drawing as a PDF anyone can open and print.

Convert DWG to PDF

Convert DXF to SVG

A DXF or DWG drawing as an SVG picture for websites and documents.

Convert DXF to SVG

Convert DWG to DXF and DXF to DWG

A drawing in the other of the two CAD formats.

Convert DWG and DXF

EDZ and parts file viewer

See the parts of an EDZ, EPLAN XML, BMEcat or CSV file as a table and save them as CSV or JSON.

Open the parts file viewer
