How to create a terminal diagram

In OpenECraft the terminal diagram is a report: it is written from the terminals of the schematic and follows every change. You draw the circuit, not the list.

See it in the live demo Open the web editor

Step by step

  1. Place terminals from the Terminals library of the Insert center and wire them. Terminals with the same tag, for example -X1, form one strip.
  2. Open Devices › Terminal strips to order the strip and to add spare terminals, jumpers, end brackets and multi-level blocks.
  3. Open Project data › Reports and pick Terminal diagram.
  4. Choose Insert as report page to put the diagram into the project. The pages update when the schematic changes.
  5. In Master data › Forms, switch the terminal diagram to its graphical form or choose its columns.

The Strips / plugs navigator shows each strip in rail order; a click shows a placed terminal in the schematic.
