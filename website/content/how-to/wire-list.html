How to generate a wire list

The wire list comes from two things you have already drawn: the connections of the schematic and the places of the devices in the panel. OpenECraft routes the wires through the ducts and measures them.

See it in the live demo Open the web editor

Step by step

  1. Draw the schematic and give the potentials their wire colour and cross-section in Connections › Potentials.
  2. Number the connections with Connections › Wire numbering.
  3. In the Panel layout workspace, place the devices on rails and the wiring ducts between them. The wires are routed through the ducts.
  4. Open Project data › Reports and pick Wire list (panel routing).
  5. Export the production wire list with File › Export, or wire from a tablet with the Wiring workspace.

The Connection list shows the connections of a schematic that has no panel layout yet.
