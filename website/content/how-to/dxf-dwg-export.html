How to export a schematic to DXF or DWG

Customers and mechanical designers often ask for drawings in their CAD format. OpenECraft writes its pages as DXF or DWG, and reads such drawings as well.

Try the exports in the live demo Open the DXF and DWG viewer

Step by step

  1. Open File › Export: it lists all exports of the project.
  2. Pick DXF or DWG. The drawing has the pages with their frames, symbols, wires and texts.
  3. To bring a drawing in, import a DXF or DWG file: it becomes the graphics of a new schematic page.

The same export is a command of the command line and the MCP server, so a script can write the drawings of every project.
