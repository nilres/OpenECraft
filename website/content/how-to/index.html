How to get common documents out of a schematic

Short guides for the documents a control cabinet needs. Each opens the live demo at the step it describes, so you can try it before you install anything.

Guides

Terminal diagram

Terminals with the same tag form a strip; the terminal diagram lists every terminal with both of its sides.

Create a terminal diagram

Wire list

Every wire with its colour, cross-section, routed length and end treatment, in order for the workshop.

Generate a wire list

Bill of materials

The parts of all devices, terminals, cables and the panel, summed into one list.

Create a bill of materials

DXF and DWG export

Schematic pages as DXF or DWG drawings for customers and other CAD programs.

Export a schematic to DXF or DWG

The tutorial walks through a whole project, from the first page to the panel layout.
