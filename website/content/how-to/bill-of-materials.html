How to create a bill of materials

Give the devices of the schematic their parts and the bill of materials is there: one list of everything to order, kept current by the project.

See it in the live demo Open the web editor

Step by step

  1. Import parts into Devices › Parts management from CSV, BMEcat, EPLAN parts XML or EDZ files, or use the example catalog.
  2. Select a device and add its parts in the Parts tab of the Properties pane. The device selection offers the parts that provide its function.
  3. Open Project data › Reports and pick Bill of materials.
  4. Export the table as CSV or Excel, or choose Insert as report page to make it part of the documents.

The bill of materials sums the parts of the devices, terminals, cables, harnesses, panel layout material, ferrules and marker material.
