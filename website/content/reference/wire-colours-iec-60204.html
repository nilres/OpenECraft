Wire colours in control cabinets

Which colour a wire of a machine's control cabinet gets after IEC 60204-1, and which colours IEC 60445 gives the conductors of cables and installations.

Wires inside the control cabinet (IEC 60204-1)

ColourAbbreviationUsed for
BlackBKPower circuits, AC and DC
RedRDControl circuits, AC
BlueBUControl circuits, DC
OrangeOGCircuits that stay live when the supply disconnecting device is off
Light blueBUNeutral conductor, where it is marked by colour alone
Green-and-yellowGNYEProtective conductor, and nothing else
  • Green-and-yellow for the protective conductor is a requirement. The other colours are the recommendation of the standard for wires marked by colour.
  • Where a neutral conductor is marked blue, blue is not used for other wires that could be mistaken for it.
  • Customers and countries have rules of their own: NFPA 79 in North America, for example, uses red for AC and blue for DC control circuits too, but white for the grounded conductor.

Conductors of cables and installations (IEC 60445)

ConductorMarkingColour
Line 1, ACL1Brown
Line 2, ACL2Black
Line 3, ACL3Grey
Neutral conductorNBlue
Protective conductorPEGreen-and-yellow
Positive, DCL+Red
Negative, DCL-White

The colours of the DC conductors are those of the edition of 2017; older installations and many control cabinets use other colours for them.

In OpenECraft a potential carries the colour and the cross-section of its wires, so the wire list, the labels and the panel in 3D show them without drawing them twice.
