IP ratings

The IP code of IEC 60529 says how well an enclosure keeps solid objects, dust and water out. The first digit stands for solid objects, the second for water.

First digit: solid objects and dust

DigitProtected againstAccess to hazardous parts
0No protectionNo protection
1Solid objects of 50 mm and moreBack of the hand
2Solid objects of 12.5 mm and moreFinger
3Solid objects of 2.5 mm and moreTool
4Solid objects of 1 mm and moreWire
5Dust in harmful amounts (dust-protected)Wire
6Dust altogether (dust-tight)Wire

Second digit: water

DigitProtected against
0No protection
1Drops falling vertically
2Drops falling vertically with the enclosure tilted up to 15°
3Spraying water at up to 60° from the vertical
4Splashing water from any direction
5Water jets from any direction
6Powerful water jets from any direction
7Temporary immersion in water
8Continuous immersion in water, under conditions the manufacturer states
9Water jets of high pressure and high temperature
  • An X in place of a digit means that this protection is not stated: IPX4 says nothing about solid objects.
  • The digits for water up to 6 include the lower ones. The digits 7, 8 and 9 do not include the water jets of 5 and 6: an enclosure that meets both is marked with both, as in IP65/IP67.
  • IP69K is a rating of ISO 20653 for road vehicles; IEC 60529 has the digit 9 since 2013.

RatingMeaningTypical use

In OpenECraft the IP rating of an enclosure is part of the data of its part, beside its size and its mounting plate.
