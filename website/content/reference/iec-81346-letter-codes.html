IEC 81346-2 letter codes

The letter of a device tag such as -K1 or -Q1 says what the device is for, not how it is built. This table lists the classes of IEC 81346-2 in the edition of 2009, which most schematics follow.

The letters and what they stand for

LetterPurpose of the objectExamples
ATwo or more purposes, none of them the main oneAssemblies, touch panels, control units
BConverting a physical quantity into a signalSensors, proximity switches, limit switches, measuring transducers
CStoring energy, information or materialCapacitors, buffer modules, memories
EProviding radiant or thermal energyLamps for lighting, heaters, cabinet heaters
FProtecting directly against dangerous or unwanted conditionsFuses, miniature circuit breakers, motor protection switches, surge arresters
GStarting a flow of energy or material, generating signalsGenerators, power supply units, batteries, fans, pumps
HProducing a new kind of material or productMixers, crushers, assembly machines
KProcessing signals or informationAuxiliary relays, timers, PLC modules, safety relays
MProviding mechanical energy for drivingMotors, actuators, solenoids
PPresenting informationSignal lamps, meters, horns, displays
QSwitching or varying a flow of energy, signals or material under controlContactors, circuit breakers, disconnectors, soft starters, valves
RRestricting or stabilising a flow or a motionResistors, diodes, chokes, check valves
SConverting a manual operation into a signalPush buttons, selector switches, emergency stop devices
TConverting energy or a signal while keeping its kindTransformers, frequency converters, rectifiers, amplifiers
UKeeping objects in a defined positionEnclosures, mounting plates, cable trays, insulators
VProcessing material or productsFilters, separators
WGuiding or transporting energy, signals or material from one place to anotherCables, wires, busbars, hoses
XConnecting objectsTerminals, terminal strips, plugs, sockets

The letters D, J, L, N, Y and Z are reserved and I and O are not used, as they look like digits.

What changed against older schematics

  • A contactor that switches a motor is -Q, not -K as in schematics after the older IEC 61346-2 and DIN 40719: -K is left for relays that process signals.
  • A signal lamp is -P, not -H, and a lamp that lights a room is -E.
  • A sensor is -B and a push button -S: who operates the device decides, not its contact.
  • A motor protection switch is -F when it protects and -Q when it is mainly used for switching.
  • The edition of 2019 arranges some classes anew. Many companies stay with the letters of 2009, and what counts in a project is that everyone uses the same.

How a device tag is put together

  • The sign before the tag names the aspect: = for the function, + for the location and - for the product, as in =A1+S1-K1.
  • After the sign of the product come the letter of the class and a number: -Q1 is the first switching device of its plant and location.
  • A connection point follows after a colon: -Q1:13 is terminal 13 of -Q1.

In OpenECraft every symbol brings its letter along, and a new device is numbered when it is placed; the structure identifiers of a page are put before the tag.
