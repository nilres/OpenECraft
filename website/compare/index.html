What OpenECraft does today

All of it is in the current release and described in the documentation.

Device-oriented schematics

Coils and contacts form one device with live cross references and contact images, structure identifiers after IEC 81346, macros with variants.

Symbol libraries

About 330 IEC 60617 symbols, NFPA, GB, GOST and JIS sets, fluid power, P&ID and installation plans, and an editor for your own symbols.

Reports and checks

Bill of materials, terminal, cable and plug diagrams as live pages, own drawn forms and a project check that follows every change.

Panel layout in 3D

Devices at real size on rails and plates, virtual wiring with duct fill levels, drilling data, enclosure climate, rooms and buildings.

Wiring and mounting assistants

The routed wires as a checklist for the workshop, with the current wire in 3D, its colour, length and ferrule: a tablet with the web editor becomes a wiring station.

Building installation

Outlets, switches and lights on the floor plan of a level, final circuits from the distribution board with cable lengths and voltage drop.

Engineering calculations

Conductor sizing, short circuit and selectivity, load calculation, EMC and safety functions after ISO 13849-1.

Data exchange

PDF, DXF, DWG and SVG; the panel as glTF, STEP, IGES, IFC and JT; connectors for ERP, PDM and PLM systems.

Open and extensible

A command line, an MCP server for AI agents and plugins in any language add checks, reports, exports and commands.

Coming from EPLAN or AutoCAD Electrical

What comes across, and what does not yet.

  • Parts: EDZ files of the EPLAN Data Portal and EPLAN XML part files are imported with their data; the 2D window macros of EDZ files become symbols and their 3D macros the 3D model of the part.
  • Drawings: DXF and DWG files are imported as graphics on a schematic page, and every page can be exported as DXF or DWG.
  • Projects: projects of EPLAN Electric P8 and AutoCAD Electrical cannot be imported yet; a project is drawn anew in OpenECraft.
  • Concepts: devices with tags, structure identifiers after IEC 81346, cross references, macros and generated report pages, as in other device-oriented tools.

Where OpenECraft is still young

  • It is a new project and has not been proven in years of production use the way the established tools have. Try it on a real project of yours before you rely on it.
  • It comes with a small set of manufacturer catalogs, far from the large data portals of the commercial vendors; parts from EDZ, BMEcat and STEP files fill the gap.
  • Its symbol libraries and editing tools grow with every release, but users of the established tools will still miss functions they are used to.
  • Help comes from the documentation and from the issue tracker of the project.
